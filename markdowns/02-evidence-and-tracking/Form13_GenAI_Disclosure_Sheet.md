## What this form actually is (so you can explain it if asked)

Form 13 is the handbook's own instrument (Appendix B, p.46) for making the AI/external-tool boundary checkable by a panel in one glance, rather than buried across a long prose log. It sits alongside `AI_Usage_Log.md` — the log is the detailed, dated, verifiable record of *what happened and when*; Form 13 is the compact, signed summary of *what was used, at what level of involvement, and what the team added beyond it*. The handbook explicitly frames it as a **contribution-boundary document**, not a confession: disclosure itself carries no penalty (§Appendix B, §C.8.4) — what's penalized is *hiding* core-assist-or-above use, or being unable to explain/justify/modify what a tool produced. The three-level category column (support / core-assist / core-replacement) is the same classification the handbook uses everywhere else in Appendix C §14: **support** needs no further justification (tooling, boilerplate, standard libraries); **core-assist** is fully permitted provided the team can justify the choice, validate its output, and explain the surrounding design; **core-replacement** — where an external component supplies the actual claimed contribution rather than assisting with it — is not acceptable and would require the contribution to be redefined or the component replaced. Every row above is deliberately in the core-assist band, matching the classification the project has already committed to elsewhere (the LLM API is documented throughout the project's other docs as "core-assist only, ~15–20% of components").
