### R7 — status note (2026-08-22): GraphTracer and FALAT checked, neither is peer-reviewed

Read in full. Both are **arXiv preprints only — not accepted or published at any peer-reviewed venue**, unlike the four kept baselines (ICML/ICLR/AAAI/ACL, all with confirmed proceedings). GraphTracer (arXiv:2510.10581) and FALAT (arXiv:2606.00765) do cover territory close to this project's core mechanism (dependency-graph tracing, counterfactual replay) and report beating AgenTracer-8B — worth continuing to monitor for whether either clears peer review before Final-2, per the standing instruction above. **Not added as baselines, not cited as established prior work, and no scope or claim in this project has been changed on the basis of either** — an unreviewed preprint is not a settled result. Re-check their publication status at each gate alongside the rest of this list.
